# BizMind BD

## AI-Powered SME Commerce, Procurement & Business Intelligence Platform

BizMind BD is a web and mobile platform designed for small and medium-sized
businesses (SMEs) in Bangladesh.

The platform combines business management, inventory, sales, supplier
management, B2B procurement, business intelligence and Agentic AI.

## Problem Statement

Many SMEs still depend on manual records, spreadsheets and personal judgment
for inventory, purchasing and business decisions.

This can lead to:

- Overstocking
- Product stockouts
- Poor supplier selection
- Difficulty forecasting demand
- Limited business analysis

## Proposed Solution

BizMind BD will provide a centralized platform where SMEs can manage their
business operations and receive AI-powered recommendations.

The Agentic AI system will analyze business data, forecast demand, compare
suppliers and prepare business actions.

## Key Features

- Sales Management
- Inventory Management
- Supplier Management
- B2B Procurement
- Business Intelligence Dashboard
- Demand Forecasting
- AI Supplier Recommendation
- AI Business Analysis
- Mobile Application
- Business Notifications

## Agentic AI

The system will use multiple specialized AI agents:

1. Demand Forecasting Agent
2. Procurement Agent
3. Supplier Intelligence Agent
4. Finance Agent
5. Business Analyst Agent

An Agent Orchestrator will coordinate these agents.
